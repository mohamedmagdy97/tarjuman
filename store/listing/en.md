Arabic subtitles for any English video. On your device, even offline.

## Full description (max 4000)
Tarjuman plays your videos and shows Arabic subtitles in sync with the English speech. Processing happens on your phone, so audio stays on your device by default, and after a one-time model download it works without internet.

What you get:
• Arabic subtitles for English videos from your files or a direct file link.
• Two modes: "Prepare, then watch" saves the subtitles for re-watching any time, and "Live" shows them while you watch.
• Accepts most common formats: MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, WMV, FLV, WEBM, TS, plus audio files like MP3, M4A and WAV.
• On-device processing: speech recognition with Whisper and translation with ML Kit, with no audio sent to a server.
• Resumes where it stopped: if the app closes mid-job, finished parts are kept.
• Subtitle editor: search and edit any line, then export an SRT file to share.
• Full control of the look: font size, colour, background, position, and an option to show English above the Arabic for language learners.
• Remembers where you stopped in each video.
• Optional online processing with your own OpenAI key. It is off by default and only runs with your consent.

Privacy in your hands:
No accounts, no ads, no tracking. Videos and subtitles stay on your device. Online mode is optional; when enabled, the video's audio is sent to OpenAI using your own key.

Good to know:
• You download the speech and translation models once from inside the app (roughly 100 to 200 MB depending on the model), then it works offline.
• Automatic translation can make mistakes, especially with fast speech, noise or specialised terms. Use the editor to fix lines.
• Only direct file links are supported. YouTube-style page links and DRM-protected content are not.
• Processing uses CPU and battery. For long videos keep the screen on and the phone charging.
